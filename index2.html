Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our clinic, procedures, and policies.

What insurance plans do you accept?

How do I prepare for my first consultation?

Please bring a valid ID, your insurance card, and any previous medical records or imaging related to your condition. Arriving 15 minutes early to fill out any necessary paperwork is recommended.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer secure telemedicine appointments for initial assessments, follow-ups, and test result reviews when a physical examination is not strictly necessary.

What is the cancellation policy?

We require at least 24 hours notice for appointment cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a cancellation fee.

How long do test results usually take?

Standard lab results are typically available within 24-48 hours. Advanced imaging and specialized tests may take 3-5 business days. Your doctor will discuss the timeline during your visit.

Is Dr. Khalil accepting new patients?

Yes, Dr. Khalil is currently accepting new patients. However, due to high demand, we recommend booking your appointment as early as possible to secure your preferred date.

Where is the clinic located?

We are conveniently located at [PLACEHOLDER_STREET_ADDRESS] in [PLACEHOLDER_CITY]. The building is fully accessible and offers complimentary valet parking for all patients.

Do I need a referral to see Dr. Khalil?

While many of our patients are referred by their primary care physicians, a referral is not always required unless mandated by your specific health insurance plan.

Are emergency appointments available?

We reserve a limited number of slots each day for urgent cases. If you are experiencing a life-threatening medical emergency, please call 911 or visit the nearest emergency room immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, cash, and bank transfers. We also offer flexible financing plans through our healthcare financing partners for out-of-pocket expenses.

Can I get a second opinion?

Absolutely. Dr. Khalil frequently provides second opinions on complex diagnoses and proposed treatment plans. Bring all relevant medical records to your consultation.

What should I expect during recovery?

Recovery timelines vary significantly depending on the specific procedure or treatment. Dr. Khalil will provide you with a detailed, personalized recovery plan and timeline prior to any intervention.
